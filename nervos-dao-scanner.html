Cell Ledger

A read-only viewer for live Nervos DAO cells. Enter a public CKB address; nothing is signed, sent or stored anywhere but your browser.

Scan an address

Never enter a private key or seed phrase. This viewer requires only a public CKB address. It cannot sign, withdraw, or move funds.
The entered address is the starting point. The scanner derives the account identifier from it and looks for DAO cells under every lock variant it can build from that identifier.

How this reads the chain

Read-only. No wallet connection, no signing, no transaction construction, no analytics, no remote logging. Requests go only to the RPC and indexer endpoints you configure. Compensation figures are estimates derived from chain data, not guaranteed returns.
